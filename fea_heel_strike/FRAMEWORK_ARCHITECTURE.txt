                          TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  FEA Setup:
    • Solver: Abaqus/Explicit (impact transient dynamics)
    • Geometry: Axisymmetric hemisphere r=40mm + rigid ground
    • Elements: CAX4R (4-node axisymmetric, reduced integration)
    • Mesh: ~2000 elements, 1mm element size
    • Contact: General contact + Coulomb friction μ=1.35
    • Load: 2.5 kg effective mass + initial velocity 0.758 m/s
    • Duration: 10 ms

  Supported Materials:
    • PLA:       E=3.5 GPa,  σ_y=60 MPa,  ρ=1240 kg/m³
