## Known Issues & Lessons Learned

### ⚠️ Catastrophic Forgetting
- **Issue**: Training beyond 200k leads to performance degradation
- **Evidence**:
  - 150k: 4.486m (-3.1% vs baseline)
  - 200k: 4.954m (+7.0%) ← recovered
  - 350k: 0.150m (-96.8%) ← catastrophic failure
- **Root cause**:
  - Reward function lacks stability penalties
  - Value network misleading policy optimization
  - Exploration-exploitation imbalance
- **Mitigation**: Use 200k checkpoint, avoid longer training

### ⚠️ Prior-Guided Architecture Limitation
- **Observation**: PPO learns action ≈ prior with minimal variance (std < 0.005)
- **Implication**: State-dependent policy barely differs from constant policy
- **Question**: Is RL necessary, or is constant residual sufficient?

### ✅ Critical Bug Fix (2026-06-08)
- **Bug**: Original V2 used `control_v136(..., self.sim_time, ...)` causing 4s failure
- **Fix**: Changed to `t = self.micro_steps * self.dt` achieving 4.630m baseline reproduction
- **Lesson**: Time parameter consistency critical for control systems
